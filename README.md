## I build and ship production SaaS — end to end, solo

Full-stack engineer and solo founder based in **Addis Ababa**. I take B2B products from blank repo to paying customers: the data model, the API, the operator console, the billing, and the compliance logic that makes the whole thing *legally usable*.

My work sits where **software meets government paperwork** — tax compliance, e-invoicing, real-estate KYC, and customs clearance. Industries where a wrong decimal is a fine, and where "good enough" isn't.

> **What that means in practice:** one engineer owns the whole stack. No handoff tax, no "that's the backend team's problem." I design it, build it, deploy it, and operate it on infrastructure I provision and pay for.
